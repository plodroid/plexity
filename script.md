# The Script

## SCENE 1 - HOOK (0:00-0:07)

- **Visual:** dark screen with one glowing video thumbnail centered. It multiplies into four copies.
- **On-screen:** "You made one video." → "Now post it four times."
- **VO:** "You made one video. Now you have to post it four times."

## SCENE 2 - THE PROBLEM (0:07-0:22)

### Beat 1
- **Visual:** tabs stack up fast (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook), each with its own upload window.
- **On-screen:** "Open every app."
- **VO:** "Open every app. Every tab. Paste the same video, again... and again."

### Beat 2
- **Visual:** a cursor copies a title, switches tabs, pastes, then does the same for the hashtags.
- **On-screen:** "Copy. Paste. Repeat."
- **VO:** "Copy the title. Paste. Copy the hashtags. Paste. One by one."

### Beat 3
- **Visual:** spinners, a failed upload, mismatched captions, a clock ticking. Everything desaturated.
- **On-screen:** "Slow. Inconsistent."
- **VO:** "It's slow. It's inconsistent. There has to be a better way."

## SCENE 3 - THE REVEAL (0:22-0:30)

- **Visual:** the clutter blurs out. A single point of violet light expands into the logo with a soft bloom.
- **On-screen:** **Argon** / "Post once. Everywhere."
- **VO:** "Meet Argon. It spreads one video to every platform, in one click."
- **SFX:** rising tone, then a soft chime as the logo locks.

## SCENE 4 - DROP IT ONCE (0:30-0:42)

- **Visual:** the Argon window floats in a tilted frame. A video file drags into the drop zone, the thumbnail glows and snaps in with a soft ripple.
- **On-screen:** "Drop it once." / "One video. That's it."
- **VO:** "Drop your video in once. That's the only time you'll have to."

## SCENE 5 - WRITE IT ONCE (0:42-0:55)

- **Visual:** match-cut to the title and description fields. Type a title and hashtags, then the four platform chips light up one by one as the text flows into each.
- **On-screen:** "Write it once." / "One title. One description."
- **VO:** "Write your title and hashtags once. Argon puts them everywhere they need to go."
- **Motion:** shift the background hue slightly to mark a new chapter.

## SCENE 6 - ONE CLICK (0:55-1:10)

- **Visual:** the cursor hovers the gradient **Spread** button, then clicks. The beam splits into four streams that fly to the four platform icons, and each lands with a checkmark and a light chime.
- **On-screen:** "One click." / "Live everywhere."
- **VO:** "Then one click. Argon spreads your video to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. Fast."
- **Hero moment:** make this the most polished shot in the video.

## SCENE 7 - 4 → 1 (1:10-1:22)

- **Visual:** a split screen. The left side is the old way (four tabs, desaturated, cluttered). The right side is Argon (one window, vivid gradient). Then three big lines animate in.
- **On-screen:** "4 apps → 1" / "4 uploads → 1" / "4 captions → 1"
- **VO:** "Four apps. Four uploads. Four captions. Now? One."

## SCENE 8 - CLOSE (1:22-1:32)

- **Visual:** the UI fades away into a full-screen gradient bloom. The logo sits centered with the tagline below, then hold for 2 seconds.
- **On-screen:** **Argon** / "Post once. Everywhere."
- **Optional small line at the bottom:** "A concept by plodroid." It isn't a CTA, but it tells viewers who made it.
- **VO:** "Argon. Post once. Everywhere."
- **Audio:** music resolves on the final line and fades out.

# Production Notes

- **No working app needed.** Build the Argon window as a Figma prototype (or a simple HTML page) and screen-record it, or animate the UI directly in After Effects, Premiere, or CapCut.
- **Icons:** use clean, simple platform glyphs for the four services rather than busy full logos, so the scene stays on-brand.
- **Pacing:** the VO is light on purpose (about 120 words). Let the animations and music carry the gaps.
- **Cuts:** for a 60-second version, trim Scene 2 to two beats and drop Scene 7. For a 9:16 version, re-frame the UI shots and enlarge the type.
